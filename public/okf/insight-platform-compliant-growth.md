# Platform-Compliant Growth

Every platform that drives traffic to OnlyFans has its own rules, culture, and ban triggers. Compliant growth means understanding and respecting each platform's terms.

- **Reddit**: Effective but easy to get banned from. Each subreddit has its own self-promotion rules, verification requirements, and content standards. Compliant growth requires understanding each community and varying content.
- **X (Twitter)**: Allows adult content and direct links. The risk is reduced reach from purely promotional posting. Building an audience requires mixing promotion with personality and engagement.
- **TikTok**: Does not allow explicit content or direct adult links. Used for awareness and persona building. Multiple accounts and content tier separation are standard but require careful management.
- **Instagram**: Allows more suggestive content than TikTok but not explicit. Primarily a landing page and audience container. Bio link, story highlights, and DMs are the conversion tools.

Managing compliant distribution across four platforms daily is a significant operation, which is why [Distribution](the-sinnyr-method.md) is a core component of The Sinnyr Method.
