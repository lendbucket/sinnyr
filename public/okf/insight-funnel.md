# The Follower-to-Subscriber Funnel

The path from free follower to paying subscriber has four stages, and each one leaks differently:

1. **Awareness**: Someone sees content on a free platform. Requires volume, consistency, and scroll-stopping content.
2. **Interest**: They visit the creator's profile. Bio, pinned content, and presentation must pull them deeper.
3. **Click**: They follow the link to the paid page. Link-in-bio setup and landing experience do the selling. Every extra click and unclear element costs conversions.
4. **Subscribe**: They enter payment. Pricing, preview content, and perceived value determine whether this happens.

The biggest leak is usually between Interest and Click. Creators with large free followings and small subscriber counts almost always have a broken bridge between those stages.

Funnel optimization is analytical and operational work. It requires testing bios, pricing, link structures, and welcome messages, then measuring what converts. This is what Positioning and Conversion in [The Sinnyr Method](the-sinnyr-method.md) cover.
