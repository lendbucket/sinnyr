# Subscriber Retention

Retention is the metric that quietly determines whether a creator's business grows or stays flat. A creator can add subscribers every week and still see the count stay the same if the churn rate matches the growth rate.

Subscribers cancel for predictable reasons: they subscribed for one promotion and never intended to stay, the content did not match expectations, posting frequency dropped, there was no engagement or relationship, or they forgot they were subscribed.

Retention is built from:

- A consistent posting schedule.
- A content mix that varies enough to maintain interest.
- Messaging and engagement that builds personal connection.
- Exclusive tiers or offers that reward long-term subscribers.
- Re-engagement campaigns for subscribers about to lapse.

Retention work is operational, repetitive, and data-driven. The [Daily Signal](the-sinnyr-method.md) tracks retention as a core metric, showing what is at risk before subscribers are lost.
