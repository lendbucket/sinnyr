# What Sinnyr Does

Every creator managed by Sinnyr receives the following as part of daily operations:

- **Platform audit and strategy**: A full review of current platforms, audience, content, pricing, and funnel, with a personalized strategy built before any work begins.
- **Profile and funnel optimization**: Bios, profile images, pinned content, welcome messages, and pricing tiers rebuilt to convert visitors into subscribers.
- **Content engine management**: One content session turned into a full week of distribution-ready assets across every platform.
- **Daily multi-platform distribution**: Managed, platform-compliant posting across TikTok, Reddit, X, and Instagram, every day.
- **Conversion and messaging strategy**: The free-to-paid funnel, DM sequences, pricing structure, and offer stack designed and managed to turn followers into paying subscribers.
- **Daily Signal**: A personalized analytics briefing delivered every day showing what is performing, what needs adjustment, and what actions to take.
- **Dedicated management team**: Direct communication and daily accountability from a team assigned to the creator.
- **Professional onboarding**: A signed management agreement, identity verification, and a structured intake process before any work begins.

More detail is available on the [What You Get](https://sinnyrmodels.com/what-you-get) page.
