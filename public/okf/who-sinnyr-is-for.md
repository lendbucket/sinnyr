# Who Sinnyr Is For

Sinnyr manages adult content creators who are serious about treating their work as a business. The agency runs a capped roster and does not accept every applicant.

The evaluation considers:

- Whether the creator is already producing content consistently, not planning to start.
- Willingness to follow a structured system.
- Professional communication and reliability.
- A business mindset about the work.
- Coachability, because [The Sinnyr Method](the-sinnyr-method.md) works when both sides commit.

There is no strict minimum subscriber count. The agency evaluates the full picture. Creators at various stages can be a fit.

When the roster is full, new applications join a waitlist. The agency does not expand capacity by lowering the standard of service.

Applying is an evaluation, not a signup. Sinnyr creates accounts by invitation only.
