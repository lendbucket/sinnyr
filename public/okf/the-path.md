# The Path

The Path is the structured onboarding journey every Sinnyr creator moves through. It has four stages with clear deliverables and milestones at each step.

1. **Intake and Audit** (Week 1): A full review of the creator's platforms, content, audience, pricing, and current numbers. Results in a personalized strategy document.

2. **Foundation** (Weeks 2 to 3): Profile, bio, content structure, pricing tiers, and the entire funnel are rebuilt to convert before any traffic is sent.

3. **Launch** (Weeks 3 to 5): Distribution starts. The content engine runs daily. The Daily Signal analytics briefing begins arriving every morning.

4. **Growth** (Ongoing): Continuous optimization based on live data. Content mix, distribution timing, messaging, pricing, and offers are refined based on real analytics.

The Path begins with an application. Sinnyr reviews every application personally and creates accounts by invitation only.
