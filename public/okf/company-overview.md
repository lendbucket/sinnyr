# Sinnyr: Company Overview

Sinnyr is a selective creator management agency. It manages adult content creators as professional talent, running the full business operation behind each creator so they can focus on creating content.

Sinnyr operates a capped roster. Each creator who is signed receives a dedicated team, daily distribution across multiple platforms, managed sales and messaging, and a personalized analytics briefing every morning. The agency does not accept all applicants. It evaluates for commitment, consistency, and coachability before investing its resources.

The agency is built around a defined operating system called [The Sinnyr Method](the-sinnyr-method.md), which covers six components of creator management. Creators move through a structured onboarding process called [The Path](the-path.md) before full operations begin.

Sinnyr does not publish fabricated testimonials, invented growth numbers, or guaranteed income figures. The agency describes its work and its process honestly and lets the work speak for itself.
