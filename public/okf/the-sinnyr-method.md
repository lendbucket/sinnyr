# The Sinnyr Method

The Sinnyr Method is the operating system behind every creator Sinnyr manages. It has six components, each designed to do work that a creator cannot sustain alone.

1. **Selection**: Sinnyr does not accept every application. The agency evaluates for commitment, consistency, and coachability before investing resources.

2. **Positioning**: Before any traffic is sent, the creator's profile, bio, content funnel, and pricing are audited and optimized so every visitor has a clear reason to subscribe.

3. **The Content Engine**: One content session is turned into a full week of platform-ready distribution assets, including clips, teasers, posts, captions, and hooks across every channel.

4. **Distribution**: An orchestrated, daily posting operation across TikTok, Reddit, X, and Instagram, managed by the Sinnyr team and built to respect each platform's rules.

5. **Conversion**: The free-to-paid funnel, messaging strategy, DM sequences, pricing structure, and offer stack are designed and managed to move followers through a structured path toward a paid subscription.

6. **Daily Signal**: Every day, the creator receives a personalized briefing built from live analytics, showing what is working, what needs adjustment, and exactly what to do next.

The method is not a course or a set of templates. It is a daily operation run by the Sinnyr team on behalf of each creator.
