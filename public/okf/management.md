# What Management Includes

Day-to-day management at Sinnyr is a full operation, not a weekly check-in. Here is what runs daily for every managed creator:

- **Content distribution**: Multiple formats posted across TikTok, Reddit, X, and Instagram, formatted and scheduled for each platform.
- **Chat and sales management**: Direct messaging, pay-per-view strategy, upsells, and subscriber engagement managed as a daily sales operation.
- **Analytics and action planning**: The [Daily Signal](the-sinnyr-method.md) delivers a personalized briefing every morning with what is working, what to adjust, and what to do next.
- **Conversion monitoring**: Funnel performance, pricing, and offer effectiveness tracked and adjusted continuously.
- **Subscriber retention**: Re-engagement campaigns, content calendars, and renewal incentives to reduce churn.
- **Platform compliance**: Monitoring rule changes across all distribution platforms to maintain reach and avoid bans.
- **Direct communication**: The creator has a dedicated team with direct access, not a queue or a chatbot.

Every creator goes through a professional onboarding process before operations begin, including a signed management agreement and identity verification. See [The Path](the-path.md) for the full onboarding journey.
