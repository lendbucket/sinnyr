# Fee Model

Sinnyr charges 12% of the creator's earnings, or $99 per month, whichever is greater.

There are no setup fees, no upfront costs, and no lock-in contracts with hidden penalties. The percentage model means the agency earns more only when the creator earns more. This aligns incentives: Sinnyr has no reason to coast, because the only way the agency gets paid well is by doing the work well.

The creator keeps 88% of earnings before platform fees. Platform fees charged by OnlyFans, Fansly, or other services are separate and set by those platforms.
