# How to Spot a Fake or Extractive Agency

The creator management space has a low barrier to entry, which means many operators are not equipped, not honest, or not structured to deliver real value. Here are the patterns that separate real management from a toll booth.

**Red flags:**

- Guaranteed income or growth numbers. No honest agency can guarantee specific figures.
- No contract or vague terms. Real management involves a written agreement with clear terms.
- Full account access with no structure or approval process.
- High upfront fees before any work is done.
- No visible system or process. If the agency cannot describe its onboarding and daily operations in detail, it does not have one.

**Questions to ask any agency:**

- What does your management agreement look like?
- How do you make money, and when do I pay?
- What does a typical day or week of management look like?
- How many creators do you manage, and how many people are on your team?
- What happens if I want to leave?
- Can you describe your onboarding process in detail?

A confident, honest firm will answer every one of these without hesitation.
